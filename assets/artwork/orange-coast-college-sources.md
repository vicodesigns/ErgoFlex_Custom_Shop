# Orange Coast College sample assets

Unmodified transparent PNG logos downloaded from Orange Coast College's official website on October 12, 2026:

- `orange-coast-college-wordmark.png`: https://orangecoastcollege.edu/assets/images/logo.png (700 × 90).
- `orange-coast-college-white-lockup.png`: https://orangecoastcollege.edu/assets/images/logo-footer.png (350 × 151).

The college's [Communications & Branding Guidelines](https://orangecoastcollege.edu/about/marketing-public-relations/newsstand-assets/docs/branding-guidelines.pdf), page 59, lists the digital colors **#002D6A** blue and **#F99239** orange. These are the Coast Navy desktop finish and Coast Orange trim in the sample configuration. Logos remain editable decal layers on the desktop and side panels.
