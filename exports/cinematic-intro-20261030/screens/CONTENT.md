# Screen content — cinematic intro (2026-10-30)

Monitor/tablet/arcade clips for the 12 chapters, mapped to the harness targets in `INVENTORY.md`.
Every clip is H.264 MP4, yuv420p, 30 fps, keyframe every 15 frames, exactly **chapter seconds + 1 s** long.
t = chapter seconds (t = 0 is the first frame of the chapter render, including its 0.75 s head handle).

Generator: `tools/experience/screens/` (deterministic canvas pages, `window.render(t)`; headless Chrome, CPU only).
- `node tools/experience/screens/capture.mjs [ids…]`: render clips (all if no ids). `--stills 1,4,8` writes PNGs to `screens/_qa/` (or `--still-dir`). `--list` prints the manifest.
- `clips.mjs` is the manifest (id, chapter, harness target, size, scene). Scenes are in `scenes/chNN.js`; the shared toolkit is in `lib.js`.
- Desk readouts are sampled from `shots/NN.json` `motion.clips` with the app's own `sampleClipMotion()`. **If a chapter's motion clips are retimed, re-run that chapter's clips** and the readouts follow automatically.
- Ch 9 is audio-reactive: `audio-bands.py` analyses the chapter's span of `score/score.wav` (start from `edl.json`), and the beat grid is 115.2 BPM from t = 0.75 s (the score's music-section bar line). Re-run ch 9 after a score or EDL change. The cache key includes the WAV's mtime.
- Ch 7 uses the app's real demo clips (`app-reference/demo_media/*_demo.mp4`), shown inverted to dark mode. Their red "incorrect form" segment (~4.8–8.1 s) is skipped.

Look: dark-mode OS/app UIs, Poppins, brand blue `#1388ec` plus vivid accents. Content was checked at its on-screen size (the inventory's max px).
