# Screen inventory — cinematic intro (2026-10-30)

Generated by `node tools/experience/render-cut.cjs --cut <12 shots> --shots all --list-screens` from the current `shots/NN.json` (ch 2 includes the new `tablet-pc`). Raw JSON per chapter: `screens/inventory/NN-screens.json`.

How to read it
- **target**: what to put in a shot's `"screens":[{"target":…}]`. Aliases: `main-landscape`, `main-portrait`, `tablet`, `tv`, `arcade`, `marquee`, `occ-display`, `media`; anything else by the listed id.
- **aspect** is the physical width/height of the glass as seen from the front (curved screens use arc length, so the picture is not stretched). Upright = world up; portrait monitors are already reported in portrait.
- **size** in mm (world units are metres). **at** = world centre [x, y, z]. **facing** = yaw of the screen normal in the XZ plane (0° = +Z, 90° = +X) and pitch (+ = tilted up).
- **in frame** = how many of 9 evenly spaced camera samples across the chapter see the front of the screen; **max px** = its largest on-screen size at 1920×1080 output. 0/9 means the current camera never sees it, so content there is optional.
- **clip** = recommended render size for the motion designer: exact aspect, enough pixels for the largest on-screen size with headroom. Any resolution at the right aspect works; the harness `cover`-fits, so a slightly different aspect only crops the edges. Use H.264 MP4 (yuv420p, keyframe every ≤ 15 frames for fast seeks), 30 fps, at least as long as the chapter plus its `offset` (clips loop otherwise).
