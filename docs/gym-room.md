# Home Gym

Preview: `index.html?room=gym&view=room&layout=spacious`.

| Room | Floor size | Ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apartment training nook | 3.2 × 4.2 m | 2.6 m |
| Dedicated home gym | 4.2 × 5.4 m | 2.8 m |
| Strength & cardio studio | 5.2 × 6.4 m | 3.0 m |
| Performance gym & recovery | 6.4 × 7.8 m | 3.2 m |
| Private wellness suite | 8.0 × 9.0 m | 3.4 m |

Charcoal rubber flooring, a ribbed MOVE wall, lime/cyan light lanes, a free
weight rack, mobility equipment, hydration cabinet and recovery bench form
the base room. Larger tiers add an indoor bike and incline bench, then an
Olympic squat rack, treadmill and freestanding boxing station. Original
equipment has physical dimensions and editable Studio groups. Reflective
wall panels use environmental reflections, without a second CAD render.

Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night and Power session change lighting and the
main desk's posture and placement. Four practical lights complement the
existing directional lights. Room choice, mode and asset edits save separately
and survive project export/import. The 48/60-inch choices change desk width;
architecture and props keep their physical size. Glide spans the floor bounds;
furniture collision avoidance is not simulated. Scene dressing stays outside
cart pricing and AR export.

Verification: `npm run test:gym`. The screenshot helper supports
`--gym-layout` and `--gym-mode`. The website package file list includes the
module; this local build does not publish it.
