# Sci-fi bay

Preview: `index.html?room=scifi&view=room&layout=spacious`.

| Room | Floor size | Ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compact ship quarters | 3.8 × 4.6 m | 2.8 m |
| Orbital crew workspace | 5.2 × 6.2 m | 3.2 m |
| Deep-space research bay | 6.8 × 8.2 m | 3.6 m |
| Exploration command & service bay | 9.0 × 10.6 m | 4.2 m |
| Orbital command & robotics hall | 12.0 × 14.0 m | 5.4 m |

Graphite deck plates, reinforced bulkheads, pale structural frames, orange
hardware and cyan/violet lighting form a space-station workspace. Original
fixtures include a mission display, crew lockers, equipment counter, access
hatch and flat illuminated floor paths. Original canvas art supplies the
orbital planet window and mission graphics; Hyperdrive adds star trails.
The graphics update when a mode changes without a continuous drawing loop.

The library radio and optics sit on a 900 mm counter. The robot dog and
Steelcase chair retain their native sizes. Larger rooms add native Kenney
space wall panels, then an original shielded research core. The command bay
adds the library airlock and factory machine alongside an original tactical
projection table. The largest hall has a native 4.8 m robot arm, cable assembly
and an original payload cradle. Oversized equipment is used only where its
physical dimensions fit; the compact room uses original smaller architecture.

Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night and Hyperdrive adjust the orbital view,
mission display, light colour/intensity, desk posture and placement. Evening
angles the standing desk for navigation. Night keeps cabin light low and
cyan desk LEDs on. Four practical lights complement overhead gantry fixtures
without adding shadow maps.

Room size and desktop width can be selected independently. Layout edits and
modes are stored per room and preserved through project export/import.
Full-floor Glide respects room boundaries; furniture collision avoidance is
not simulated. Inspiration props do not affect pricing or AR export.
