# Director workbench — 2026-10-26

## References and choices

Reviewed the official documentation for:

- [Adobe Premiere timeline navigation](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/edit-projects/change-clip-sequence/navigation-controls-in-the-timeline.html): a ruler, visible playhead, precise position entry, and zoom navigation. Applied a single seconds ruler shared by camera and desk tracks, an exact seconds field, horizontal zoom, and a scrollable view.
- [Unreal Engine Sequencer](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/sequencer-cinematic-editor-unreal-engine): named tracks, sections, keyframes, snapping controls, and transport navigation. Applied dedicated Camera/Lift/Tilt/Wheels rows, nearby edit snapping, and previous/next edit buttons.
- [Blender timeline manual](https://docs.blender.org/manual/id/dev/animation/animation_editors.html): a common playhead and frame navigation. Applied ruler scrubbing, keyboard frame stepping and Home/End navigation. The English timeline page could not be fetched; the official animation-editor manual was available through search.

The editor keeps camera keyframes distinct from timed desk movement blocks. Camera and desk tracks share the same horizontal coordinates, ruler, zoom and playhead.

## Workflow

- The inspector has **Project**, **Chapter**, **Camera**, and **Groove** sections. Selecting a camera diamond or movement block opens and scrolls to its relevant settings.
- Project contains versions, saved client cuts, cut generation, import/export/link and soundtrack volume. Chapter contains scene/build/activity, text, duration and bake settings.
- The chapter strip remains separate from the chapter's tracks. Its toggle hides the strip while working on a chapter. The inspector toggle expands the preview; selecting a timeline item brings its controls back. On narrow screens the inspector starts hidden.
- **Titles** toggles preview captions while editing. Leaving Director Mode restores normal presentation captions.
- Click or drag the shared ruler to scrub. The exact playhead field accepts chapter seconds. Previous/next edit jumps among camera views and movement edges.
- Zoom in/out from Fit to 8×. All tracks zoom together. The horizontal scrollbar pans the enlarged timeline; labels remain at the left edge. Fit restores the complete chapter.
- Snap settings: Off, 0.1s, 0.5s and 1s. While dragging camera keys or movement edges, nearby camera keys, clip edges, playhead and chapter boundaries attract within eight screen pixels. Alt bypasses snapping. Same-lane overlap is still rejected.
- Drag the top timeline handle to change the preview/timeline balance; Up/Down adjusts the focused handle. Tracks scroll within the timeline when space is limited.
- Space plays/pauses the chapter. +/− zoom. On the ruler or empty timeline, Left/Right steps 1/30 second, Shift steps one second, and Home/End seeks to the chapter boundaries. On a selected key or movement, existing nudge/delete shortcuts remain. Ctrl/Cmd+Z and Shift+Ctrl/Cmd+Z work while the timeline is focused.
- Original wheel motion splits at direction reversals and stops: chapter 1 has forward 0–4s and backward 4–8s. Each retains its original curve and simultaneous heading change. The earlier combined original card also splits when opened without rewriting saved cuts until an edit is made.
- Adding a movement fits a shorter available gap when a three-second default block will not fit. Fully occupied lanes need a shorter block, a moved block or a longer chapter.
