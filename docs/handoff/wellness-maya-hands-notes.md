# Wellness (ch07): Maya's hands through the keyboard during her look-over

- Branch: `director/wellness-maya-hands`
- Base: c872e81a (`origin/video/cinematic-intro-hq`, fetched explicitly).
- Studio state: the committed `studio-state.json`, rev 9671. Renders used `--storage studio-state.json`.
- Scope: 3rd cut only. Cast 1-3, the camera, the music, `score/`, `edl.json` and `score-pins.json` are untouched.

## Cause

`maya-stand` (`people-maya-stand-type-41`) has pose keys at 9.1, 9.35, 9.55, 9.75 and 10.1 s. They key only `spine_03`, `neck_01` and `head`:

- `spine_03` turns about 7.5 deg of yaw plus a little pitch and roll, peaking at 9.55 s.
- The arms are not keyed, so they ride along with the chest.
- The motion pins her hands on the keys before the pose keys turn on top (`drawPose`: `bone.q = rest * key`). The chest turn therefore carries both hands about 34 mm sideways and drops them.

Headless measurements at 9.55 s, against the key-less motion:

- Hand and fingertip joints are up to 57.8 mm off (ring_03_l).
- They sit 23.7 mm lower.
- Skin points sink up to 19.5 mm (left) and 26.3 mm (right) deeper below the key tops than in the as-built pose.
- Both palms enter the keyboard tray plate (Mesh_3), 76 and 73 points.

The close strips show the hands sliding and the fingers sinking into the keys from about 9.2 to 10.0 s.

## Change (pose keys only)

The same 5 key times now also key `upperarm`, `lowerarm` and `hand`, left and right. These are counter-turns:

- At each key time the script loads the key-less motion pose and applies the existing spine, neck and head turn (`poseAt`).
- It then runs the rig's own two-bone `reach` twice to bring each wrist back to the motion's wrist, and `setWorld` on the hand to restore the motion's hand orientation. This is the same construction as `handsKey` in room-people-motion.mjs.
- Key = `motionLocal^-1 * wantedLocal`, rounded to 1e-4.
- Script: `wellness-maya-hands/design.mjs`.

Key sizes: upperarm 7.9-8.7 deg, lowerarm 0-8.3 deg, hand 5.4-6.1 deg. The upperarm stays far below the 26 deg at which `poseTapWeight` starts suppressing typing. 9.1 and 10.1 are identity, so nothing changes before 9.1 or after 10.1. The spine, neck and head keys are unchanged, and so is the ease (default smooth).

The fingers are children of the hand, so they keep the motion's pose too. The shoulders still follow the chest; the elbows give a little (at most 0.93 mm per frame at 120 fps).
