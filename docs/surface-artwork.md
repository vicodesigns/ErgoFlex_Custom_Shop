# Premium artwork and wraps

Open **Desktop finish → Premium artwork & wraps** in Store or Product & finishes.

1. Choose a part from the grouped **Desk surface** menu: desktop and side panels; three shelves; desktop front crossbar; footrest/footer crossbar; shelf back and side panels; outer leg panels; left/right feet; and the upper, middle and lower sections of both lift columns. There are 21 printable parts. **Pick in 3D** selects the part you click; **View surface** frames it.
2. Choose **Graphic / logo** or **Texture**, then add one or several PNG, JPG or WebP files (up to 12 MB each). Sample thumbnails also add an image. Transparent PNG/WebP retains the finish around a graphic.
3. Choose an image from **Edit image**, then drag the flat preview or use horizontal/vertical position, scale and rotation. Layout supports a single image, repeating grid, offset rows and fill surface. Pattern spacing and opacity are independent per image. **Replace selected image** retains its placement; **Remove selected image**, directly below the selector, removes only that image and automatically saves the change.
4. Choose **Full surface wrap** or **Removable, reusable decal** for each layer. A texture wrap and a logo decal can coexist on the same part.

Each surface supports up to 8 graphics and 8 textures, with independent transforms and visibility. Textures render first, then graphics in menu order. Actual mesh triangles clip them at the outline and cut-outs. Wraps include the edges and reverse face using box projection; decals use the upper face on horizontal surfaces and outward face on side panels. Layers follow lift, tilt and glide and receive scene lighting; LED spill is drawn above them. Trim and hardware retain their own materials. This is a visualization, not production print tooling or an assertion that a chosen material has been tested for reusability. Print fit, wrap seams and decal material are confirmed in the premium quote.

Artwork auto-saves to this browser in IndexedDB. On an ordinary reload, the latest artwork draft takes precedence over artwork in an older saved build; a shared build link keeps its explicit configuration. **Save build** embeds the image assets and placements with the configuration. Saved builds, cart previews and project exports preserve them; old builds without artwork still load. A project restore or Reset build replaces the artwork as part of the configuration. **Download artwork / Import artwork** transfers the recipe separately. **Share build** downloads a full project when it contains artwork, avoiding an oversized URL. Local browser drafts do not sync between devices.

Uploads are decoded locally and rasterized to WebP with transparency (up to 1536 pixels on the long side, reduced further if needed). Each embedded image is limited to 700,000 characters; the whole recipe is limited to 3,600,000. Keep originals for print production. Remote image URLs and SVG/HTML uploads are not accepted. Failed uploads retain the previous layer. Storage failures leave the editor usable and ask the user to download artwork.

`artwork-config.mjs` owns normalization and portable validation. `surface-artwork.mjs` owns the editor, image processing, persistence and mesh overlays. `catalog.mjs` preserves artwork in `cleanConfig()` and adds quoted premium lines without inventing a price. `studio.js` binds surfaces in the neutral product frame after motion rigs load and rebinds after a desktop-size change. GPU overlay geometry, textures and materials are disposed when re-bound or the model reloads.

## Cal Poly Humboldt samples

The gallery offers two transparent wordmarks: white “Humboldt” lettering and the original green lettering. “Cal Poly” stays teal in both. The full sample desk uses the white version. Sample clicks add images: remove the old wordmark if you only want to change its color. Desktop finishes offer both the original Forest Green (`#004c46`) and a darker, earthier Earthy Forest Green (`#203b2b`).

The editor includes the customer's supplied wordmark and university seal. Choose Desktop and click the wordmark and seal thumbnails to add both, then choose each under **Edit image** and position it. **Apply Humboldt desk** sets Forest Green / Black with gold trim, replaces the first graphic on the desktop and each side panel with the default wordmark/seals, and retains additional images, other surface artwork and textures. All placements remain editable and use the normal artwork autosave/project export paths. The white wordmark was edited in GIMP; its transparency and teal “Cal Poly” are preserved. Previously saved images remain embedded in their builds. The supplied `.svg` seal file actually contains WebP bytes, so the bundled sample is correctly named `.webp`.

Version 1 recipes keep their original `logo` and `texture` keys, with additional keys `logo-2` through `logo-8` and `texture-2` through `texture-8`. Normalization, validation and project exports preserve every image. Removing an image disposes its GPU overlay. The existing total embedded-image budget also applies to multi-image recipes.

New receivers bind to the named source meshes in `bindSurfaceArtwork()`. Front crossbars project onto +X faces and the rear shelf panel onto −X; side and leg decals project onto the outward ±Z face. Feet use their top face. Each overlay is parented to its own part, so telescoping column sections follow their individual lift ratios. Wrap projection covers the whole selected mesh, including reverse faces and edges. The preview uses the selected part's material color. Wheels, actuators, screws, electronics and LED emitters remain hardware rather than printable panel options.
