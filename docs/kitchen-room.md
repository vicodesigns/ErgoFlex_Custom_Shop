# Kitchen

Preview: `index.html?room=kitchen&view=room&layout=spacious`.

| Room | Floor size | Ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Home kitchen & coffee corner | 4.4 × 5.2 m | 2.8 m |
| Island kitchen & dining room | 5.6 × 6.2 m | 3.0 m |
| Entertaining kitchen & dining | 6.8 × 7.6 m | 3.2 m |
| Chef’s kitchen & gathering suite | 8.2 × 9.0 m | 3.4 m |

Oak cabinetry, jade tile, brass fittings and pale stone create a warm kitchen.
Original cabinet geometry keeps worktops at 900 mm, with an inset sink,
induction range and hood. Library appliances retain their physical size.
The smallest room includes a breakfast banquette; larger rooms add a coffee
corner, dining chairs, a waterfall island and an open display pantry.

Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night and Dinner party adjust daylight, four
practical lights, desk placement, posture and LEDs. The desk becomes a recipe,
prep or serving station. Each room saves its own edits and presentation.
Desktop width and room size are independent. Glide covers the floor bounds;
furniture collision avoidance is not simulated. Scenery is excluded from cart
pricing and AR export.

Verification: `npm run test:kitchen`. The screenshot helper supports
`--kitchen-layout` and `--kitchen-mode`. The website package file list includes
the module; local changes do not publish it.
