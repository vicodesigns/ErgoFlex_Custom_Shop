# Contemporary lounge · curated interior revision

The contemporary lounge received a construction/detail pass, an independent specialist-agent critique of actual application renders, and a further arrangement/lighting revision. The Earthy lounge remains available as the existing comparison style.

## Changes

- One dominant original coastal painting, lowered over the sofa; different code-authored graphic studies around the desk and credenza.
- Open walnut shelving and a styled book cabinet with real shelf contacts, picture lights, ceramics and a shaded lamp.
- Floor-length linen curtain stacks outside the glazing; the complete bar retains its measured window clearance.
- Cognac leather, colored upholstery, staggered walnut flooring, subtle plaster relief, honed stone and restrained metal finishes.
- A reading table/lamp and three-globe opal pendant cluster; visible light sources and lower localized hot spots.
- The loose lounge chair joins the sofa/coffee table instead of standing next to the TV. Untouched old chair/plant anchors migrate; moved, rotated or scaled owner items retain their edits.
- The social table has a dark felt inset and an actual 3D mid-game chess setting.
- Premium and Executive use authored 98-inch and 110-inch cinema displays, with broader timber-framed media walls.

## Review

The first specialist review rejected the first pass for overly hot sofa lighting, repetitive art, isolated seating, a conference-like table and an undersized TV. Those findings drove the revisions above. Final images and checks are recorded alongside this file.

The screenshot harness now explicitly enables the ceiling for interior views (the cinematic adapter intentionally hides it by default), uses above-ceiling cameras for cutaways and preserves the native viewport aspect ratio in its review images. A deferred-image regression test ensures the painted artwork updates the live material copied by the room-lighting shader.

## Verification scope

Node geometry checks cover all ten style/layout combinations, shelf support contacts, coffee-table support elevation, window margins, practical-light counts, no added practical shadow maps, deferred artwork, scene-desk persistence and project validation. Browser inspection covers the authored layouts and activities. Bounds/overlap reports are conservative geometry checks; they do not validate arbitrary later owner edits or replace dynamic collision planning.


## Final specialist review

The reviewer signed off on the complete Premium seating and overview views
in `approved/`. The revised sofa, coffee table and armchair form a conversation
group; the larger cinema and chess table establish lounge activities; and the
single painting plus graphic studies give the walls a coherent hierarchy.
No further design blocker was reported for these reviewed views. The reviewer
recommended keeping the remaining floor open for circulation.

The review also prompted stronger walnut grain and knot variation. Intermediate
capture diagnostics were moved out of the project; the approved files are the
complete browser-rendered views intended for sharing.


Final browser matrix: **100 states checked**, with no detected out-of-room items, floor-furniture bounding-box overlaps or missing props. The minimum measured bar/window gap was **205 mm**. See `verification-summary.json`. The matrix covered both styles, all five room sizes, both desk widths and all five activities.
