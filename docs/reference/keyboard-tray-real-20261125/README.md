# Vico's real keyboard tray bracket, photographed 25 Nov 2026 (about 13:03 and 13:12)

Eleven photos (resized to 1800 px) of the real tray on Vico's own desk, to guide the articulated tray rig (see docs/keyboard-tray/SPEC.md).
They show how the real mount moves. Vico: "photos of my real bracket and how I am able to move it around."
The unit is an Uncaged Ergonomics under-desk tray (label visible in some photos).

What Orch saw (verify against the CAD, do not trust this blindly):
* Top views: the empty platform, black, two velcro strips and screw holes; keyboard and mouse sit on the desktop shelf above (the tilted-desk case).
* Underside views: the platform hangs from a long flat ARM (link). At its rear end the arm is bolted to an L-shaped bracket that is screwed to the desk underside (two bolts through the bracket: this is the base pivot Vico means).
* At the front end of the arm there is a plate with a curved (arc) SLOT and a lock bolt running in it: the platform's own tilt adjustment (surface tilt), with the label on this plate.
* Silver slide rails run along the underside above the arm (the slide joint); a swivel stud near the middle (the swing).
* Several photos show different poses: platform out and level, arm angled, platform folded up under the desk.
Use them to place the pivots exactly (which bolt is the base pivot, where the arc slot ends are, how far the arm really turns, what the tucked pose looks like).
