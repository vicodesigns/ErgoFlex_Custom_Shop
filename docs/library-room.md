# Library

Preview: `index.html?room=library&view=room&layout=house`.

This is a campus-library concept for the student ErgoFlex pilot. It is not a
measured reconstruction of the Cal Poly Humboldt library. Photos, a floor plan
or dimensions of the intended area can be used to adapt it to that location.

| Room | Floor size | Ceiling | Bookcases |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Student pilot study nook | 5.4 × 6.6 m | 3.0 m | 1 |
| Campus reading room | 7.2 × 9.0 m | 3.1 m | 4 |
| Learning commons | 10.0 × 12.0 m | 3.4 m | 7 |
| University study hall | 13.0 × 16.0 m | 3.8 m | 13 |
| Flagship library commons | 18.0 × 20.0 m | 4.4 m | 22 |

The active student desk sits beside large windows. Warm oak shelves, muted
green carpet, quiet reading seating, shared study tables and a redwood view
create the campus setting. Larger rooms add double-sided book stacks and
window study carrels. Original signage identifies the student pilot and the
commons without presenting an official university brand or measured site.

Shelves use six instanced book materials to keep large collections affordable
to render. Study tables are 740 mm high; native laptops, books and journals
rest on their surfaces. Native Steelcase and meeting chairs retain their
physical dimensions when desktop width changes. Four practical lights add no
shadow maps. Window art redraws on mode changes without a drawing loop.

Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Late study and Study group change daylight,
reading lights, posture and active-desk position. Room size and desk width are
independent after initial selection. Edits and mode choices persist per layout
and through project export/import. Full-floor Glide respects the room's outer
boundaries; furniture collision avoidance is not simulated. Scene furniture
does not affect the desk purchase price or AR export.

Verification: `npm run test:library`. The screenshot helper supports
