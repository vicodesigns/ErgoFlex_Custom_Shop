# Early-years room balance review

A fresh early-childhood-focused review found the floor routes generally clear, but too many competing wall displays. This pass keeps warmth and personal belongings while giving the eye somewhere to rest.

## Review basis

The specialist agent reviewed the actual five room stills and the room, activity and wall code. Its recommendations were informed by [Head Start's infant/toddler environment guidance](https://headstart.gov/learning-environments/article/designing-classroom-socialization-environments-infants-toddlers), which discusses quiet areas, circulation and visual stimulation, and [NAEYC's toddler environment guidance](https://www.naeyc.org/our-work/families/what-does-high-quality-program-for-toddler-look-like), which describes separate interest areas, open movement space and familiar pictures at children's viewing height. Applying classroom guidance to these illustrated bedrooms is a design inference, not a professional certification or a fixed age prescription.

## Changes

- One detailed play-wall mural; the rear wall becomes a low-contrast landscape wash. Storybook towers, animals and village buildings no longer repeat around the sleeping area.
- One collected child-art display replaces the layered right poster gallery, rear framed pair, drying rail and extra project gallery. A small framed, word-free bedtime picture stays over the bed.
- Short fabric bunting stays on the play side, with 5–9 flags rather than 9–17 across the entire wall.
- A low family drawing sits beside the book pocket; the higher flower painting clears the pocket and books. Larger wardrobes keep one personal drawing at a lower height.
- Front-facing book choices reduce from eight to four in the Little noticer room and six in the other early-years rooms.
- The Little noticer morning invitation has three large shapes, without another sorting tray competing beside them.
- The inventor's afternoon bridge remains an ongoing floor project. Other profiles keep that mat open during drawing time.
- Secondary kitchen and little-world materials remain put away outside their relevant session. The Story makers house remains active for its own morning/afternoon story, while Nature & rhythm emphasizes its listening bench.

## Verification

The child-room geometry checks passed for all 25 age/size recipes across all five activities, including authored furniture clearances, desk routes, grounding, stage persistence and finish ownership. Added checks cover the single detailed mural, display consolidation, reduced book selection, low picture clearance from the pocket/books, and correctly stored secondary activity kits. `npm run test:environment-finishes`, child-screen tests and scene-desk data tests passed. Browser evidence for all 25 early-years size/activity states is saved alongside this document. Each reported the correct activity, one detailed mural, one display cluster, and supported desktop kits with zero reported overlap.

The specialist agent then reviewed the revised Little noticer afternoon, Story makers afternoon and Nature & rhythm night stills. It found no visible material regression and recommended retaining this balance of quiet sleeping walls, expressive play walls and open circulation. Remaining changes should focus on close material quality or owner-specific choices rather than more decoration.

Textures remain generated once per built room and owned by that room. This pass adds no lights, animation loops or per-frame drawing.
