## Shape
The finale is **eight desks with hard cuts on the beat**. The cuts come every bar, then every half bar, into the red desk.
The red desk then **holds** under the closing title, the wordmark and "Let's build yours."

- Grid: 112 BPM. One beat is 0.536 s, one bar is 2.14 s.

## Rules kept
- No desk is seen as a bare tilted board. Every desk is posed before its cut and shown fully dressed.
  - Auto-stow only hides items above 9 deg. Above 9 deg are the artist at 65 (easel stays) and the architect at 22 (kit stays).
- Desk looks travel as bundles, as in S18: preset, lights, sceneEdits, screens, exposure, accessories.
  - Library and music are new looks, built from the office and hbhs bundles.
- One desk move and one camera are still shared by all sub-shots and the cut's hero.
  - The desk move jumps to the next pose in the last 0.02 s before each cut.
  - The camera is one spline with `cut` keys, so the score also sees the cuts.
